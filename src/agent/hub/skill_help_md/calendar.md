**Calendar Skill**

The Calendar skill helps you manage events, reminders, and your schedule.

**What it helps with**
- Viewing your agenda for today or this week
- Creating, updating, and deleting events
- Finding free time slots
- Setting reminders and recurring events
- Checking conflicts in your calendar

**Try asking things like**
- What's on my calendar tomorrow?
- Create a meeting with Rahul at 4 PM on Friday
- Find a free 30-minute slot this afternoon

**Common mistakes**
- Include a date and time if you want me to create an event.
- Say how long the event should be if you want me to find a slot.
- Mention attendees if the event involves other people.
