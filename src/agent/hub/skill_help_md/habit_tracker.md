**Habit Tracker Skill**

The Habit Tracker skill helps you build routines and track your progress over time.

**What it helps with**
- Creating and deleting habits
- Logging daily habit completions
- Showing your daily check-in and pending habits
- Tracking current and longest streaks
- Generating weekly and long-term progress reports
- Optionally scheduling habit sessions on your calendar

**Try asking things like**
- Add a habit to drink water every day
- Mark meditation as done today
- Show my habit streaks for this week

**Common mistakes**
- Say the habit name clearly when logging progress.
- If you want a report, mention the time period like today, this week, or this month.
- If you are creating a new habit, say what the habit is and how often you want to do it.
