**LinkedIn Skill**

The LinkedIn skill helps you create, manage, and analyze LinkedIn content.

**What it helps with**
- Creating LinkedIn posts
- Scheduling content for later
- Generating post ideas and draft text
- Tracking post and page analytics
- Managing published and scheduled posts

**Try asking things like**
- Draft a LinkedIn post about product leadership
- Schedule this post for Monday 9 AM
- Show analytics for my last 5 LinkedIn posts

**Common mistakes**
- Say whether you want to draft, post, schedule, or analyze content.
- If you want a post written, mention the topic and tone.
- If you want analytics, say which post or what date range you mean.
