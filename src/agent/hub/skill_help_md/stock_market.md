**Stock Market Skill**

The Stock Market skill helps you understand stocks and portfolios in a read-only way.

**What it helps with**
- Getting stock prices and recent market data
- Technical indicators like RSI, MACD, and Bollinger Bands
- Risk and volatility analysis
- Portfolio comparison and diversification insights
- Sentiment and report generation

**Try asking things like**
- Analyze Apple stock
- Compare TCS and Infosys
- Generate a stock report for Nvidia

**Common mistakes**
- Mention the stock name or ticker clearly.
- Say if you want a quick answer, comparison, or full report.
- This skill is for analysis only, not buying or selling.
