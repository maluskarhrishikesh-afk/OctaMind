**Email Skill**

The Email skill helps you handle your email faster and with less effort.

**What it helps with**
- Reading, searching, and counting emails
- Sending, replying to, and forwarding messages
- Creating drafts before sending
- Summarizing long email threads
- Downloading and working with attachments
- Organizing email with labels and categories

**Try asking things like**
- Show me unread emails from today
- Reply to John's email and say I'll send the report by 5 PM
- Summarize the last 10 emails from my manager
- Review my mailbox
- Apply mailbox cleanup now
- Show my mailbox preferences

**Common mistakes**
- Say who the email is for if you want me to send or reply to one.
- Mention the subject or sender if you want me to find a specific email.
- If you want an attachment handled, mention the file name if you know it.

**Mailbox cleanup guidance**
- If you want mailbox cleanup, say `mailbox`, `inbox`, or `email` explicitly.
- After a mailbox review, `apply cleanup now` should work as an email follow-up.
- If the request could mean mailbox cleanup or file cleanup, I should ask you to clarify instead of guessing.
